1. Introduction & Executive Summary

Welcome to AsciiLine: Video To Ascii (referred to as "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), engineered and published by VirelonSoft.

This Privacy Policy describes the foundational security protocols, local processing frameworks, and operational commitments governing your use of the Application. We consider privacy not merely as a regulatory requirement, but as a core design principle embedded directly into our codebase.

By downloading, installing, or operating AsciiLine: Video To Ascii, you agree to the transparent principles established in this document.

2. Local Architecture & 100% Offline Processing

Unlike traditional cloud-based video converters that demand uploading personal footage to remote data centers, AsciiLine: Video To Ascii operates completely on your physical hardware:

  • Native C++ Engine: Frame extraction, color-space mapping, luminance calculation, and character-matrix conversion are executed using highly optimized native machine code on your device CPU and GPU.
  • Zero Cloud Infrastructure: VirelonSoft does not operate any video or image ingestion servers. No media packets are streamed, cached, or analyzed off-device.
  • Air-Gapped Operation: You can operate the entire creative studio in Airplane Mode without any loss of functionality.

4. Information We Do NOT Collect

VirelonSoft adheres to an absolute Zero-Data-Retention model:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We never collect your name, phone number, email address, or billing details.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not capture IMEI, MAC address, Android ID, or Google Advertising ID (GAID).
  • No Geolocation: GPS and network triangulation are completely disabled and unrequested.
  • No Background Clipboard Snooping: The clipboard is modified only when you manually tap "Copy to Clipboard".
  • Zero Data Monetization: We do not sell, barter, or distribute telemetry to data brokers or advertising networks.

5. Third-Party Integrations & SDKs

The Application contains no invasive ad networks (such as AdMob, Meta Audience Network, or Unity Ads) and no behavioural analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics or AppsFlyer).

Standard Android OS services provided by Google Play Services (e.g., standard OS crash logs and Google Play licensing verification) operate according to Google's standard privacy guidelines: Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data Ownership, Retention & Erasure

You maintain 100% intellectual property and sovereign ownership over all creations produced with AsciiLine:

  • All rendered outputs are stored strictly within your device's private sandbox or your chosen public gallery album.
  • You may delete rendered files at any time through your Android Files or Photos app.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately destroys all internal temporary buffers and cached frames.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

AsciiLine is a creative utility suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit information from children under 13 (or under 16 within the EEA). Because our architecture collects zero data from all users universally, the Application provides a safe, non-commercial environment fully compliant with COPPA regulations.

8. International Compliance (GDPR, CCPA & KVKK)

Under major international privacy legislation (European GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, and Turkish KVKK):

  • Right to Access: Your data is solely on your device and instantly accessible to you.
  • Right to Erasure: Simply deleting your files or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local traces.
  • No Data Sale: We never sell personal data as defined under the CCPA or GDPR.
