01 // OVERVIEW & PHILOSOPHY

Introduction and Core Architecture

This Privacy Policy describes how Virelonsoft ("we", "our", or "company") treats information collected, processed, and maintained through the mobile application 5D KLIK (the "Application").

Unlike traditional modern applications that harvest behavioral metrics, 5D KLIK follows a strict Privacy-by-Design ethos. The application is completely functional without requiring any registration, telephone number, email, or third-party social login.

02 // DATA COLLECTION SPECIFICATIONS

Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly state that 5D KLIK does NOT collect, store, transmit, sell, or rent any of the following categories of data:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or government IDs.
  • Device Fingerprinting & Identifiers: No Advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or persistent hardware identifiers.
  • Location Data: No GPS, Wi-Fi tri-angulation, or cellular tower location data is ever accessed or requested.
  • Biometrics & Camera/Microphone: 5D KLIK does not request or possess permissions to access your camera, microphone, or biometric sensors.
  • Financial Information: We do not process or store credit cards, bank accounts, or billing data.
03 // ON-DEVICE STORAGE

Local Data Storage (MMKV & Shared Preferences)

All your reading preferences, study streaks, bookmarked micro-lessons, and completion timestamps are stored exclusively on your local hardware device using ultra-fast, encrypted MMKV storage.

This data remains sandboxed inside your operating system’s secure application storage container. If you uninstall the application or clear app storage via Android Settings, all local data is immediately deleted. We have no remote copy, backup, or ability to access this data.

04 // NETWORK ACCESS & EXTERNAL SERVICES

Public Archival Feeds (Wikimedia API)

The application contains an optional live archival radar component ("On This Day") that fetches public historical milestone events.

  • Provider: Wikimedia REST API (api.wikimedia.org)
  • Purpose: Querying public, read-only encyclopedic events for the current calendar date.
  • Payload: Only the current month and day digits (e.g. /08/22) are transmitted. No user identifiers, cookies, or tracking headers are included.
  • Offline Mode: When offline, 5D KLIK utilizes a rich built-in local database with zero network calls.
05 // DEVICE PERMISSIONS

Device Permissions & Native Features

5D KLIK requests only the minimum native permissions necessary to provide standard OS features:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional permission used solely to trigger local, scheduled on-device reminders (e.g. Daily 5-minute study alert). Notifications are generated locally by your phone's operating system without communicating with any push notification server.
  • APP_WIDGET: Used to render the Android Home Screen Widget (2x2 and 4x2) directly from your phone's internal storage.
  • VIBRATE & HAPTICS: Used to deliver tactile sensory feedback during button presses and quiz evaluations.
06 // COMPLIANCE & RIGHTS

GDPR, CCPA & Children's Privacy Compliance

Because 5D KLIK does not collect, process, or transmit personal data to remote servers, our application inherently complies with major international data privacy frameworks, including the European Union GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, and Turkish KVKK.

Children’s Online Privacy Protection (COPPA): 5D KLIK contains no harmful content and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 or any age group.
