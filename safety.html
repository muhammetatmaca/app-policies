Child Safety Standards & CSAE/CSAM Prevention Policy

Official public standards of Virelonsoft for SwanPulse : Social Media. This charter articulates our absolute zero-tolerance protocol regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), grooming prevention, and mandatory escalation to international law enforcement.

1. Zero-Tolerance Absolute Prohibition

Virelonsoft maintains a strict, unconditional zero-tolerance policy against any form of child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or child sexual abuse material (CSAM/CSAE). Any attempt to create, upload, solicit, distribute, transmit, or facilitate content depicting or referencing the exploitation of minors results in immediate and permanent account termination, cryptographic key revocation, asset purging, and mandatory reporting to criminal authorities.

Prohibited Conduct Includes:
  • Any photographic, illustrative, digital, AI-generated, or synthetic depictions of child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Grooming behaviors, predatory communication, sexualized solicitation of minors, or age-inappropriate contact.
  • Sharing or linking to external domains, forums, or storage buckets hosting illicit or abusive material.
  • Financial exploitation or extortion targeting individuals under the legal age of majority.

2. In-App Reporting & Rapid Flagging Mechanisms

SwanPulse equips all users with intuitive, accessible, and confidential reporting channels built directly into every post, profile, and conversation:

  • Post-Level Reporting: Users can tap the options menu (three dots) on any pulse, image, or comment and select "Report > Child Safety Concern".
  • Profile-Level Reporting: Suspicious accounts can be reported directly from the user profile card.
  • Priority Escalation Queue: Any report categorized under child safety bypasses standard queues and is instantly flagged as Severity-1 Emergency, routed directly to designated safety personnel for immediate human review.
  • Direct Confidential Helpline: Users, parents, and legal guardians can report emergency concerns directly via email at muhammetatmaca79@gmail.com.

3. Response Timelines & Enforcement SLA

Upon receiving a notification or flagging an instance of potential CSAE/CSAM:

  1. Immediate Quarantine (< 1 Hour): The reported content is immediately quarantined from all public and private feeds, and CDN distribution nodes are cleared.
  2. Forensic Preservation: Cryptographic hashes, IP access stamps, and relevant device metadata are preserved in an isolated, encrypted evidentiary vault for law enforcement inspection.
  3. Permanent Account Banishment: The offending account is permanently banned, and hardware device fingerprints are blacklisted to prevent re-registration.

4. Mandatory Law Enforcement & NCMEC Reporting

In strict compliance with 18 U.S.C. § 2258A, EU Child Safety Directives, and Turkish Criminal Code (TCK Md. 226):

Virelonsoft fulfills all statutory duties to report any identified instances of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and child exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the automated CyberTipline portal. Furthermore, our legal department cooperates proactively with regional and international law enforcement agencies, including INTERPOL, Europol, and local cybercrime directorates (EGM Siber Suçlarla Mücadele).
