Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Virelonsoft collects, stores, and protects your information when you use FootIQ: Football Logic Puzzles (com.virelon.futbolanaliz).

01

Introduction & Architecture Overview

Welcome to FootIQ ("we", "our", or "the App"), proudly created and operated by Virelonsoft. We believe in absolute user privacy and data minimization.

⚡ 100% Offline-First Design: FootIQ operates locally on your mobile device. The comprehensive football career graph, player database, and puzzle mechanics run inside a local, bundled SQLite database. We do NOT operate remote tracking servers, and we never sell your personal data.

02

Information We Collect & How It Is Used

FootIQ collects only minimal, non-sensitive operational data strictly required to deliver the core game experience:

  • Local Game Progress & High Scores: Solved mystery cases, highest streaks in Higher/Lower, solved 11 formations, and player badges are saved exclusively on your local device via encrypted SQLite/AsyncStorage.
  • User Interface Preferences: Your chosen UI language (among the 20 supported languages), audio/haptics toggle, and pitch board preferences.
  • Technical Diagnostics & Crash Logs: If a crash occurs, standard non-identifiable system diagnostics (such as device model, OS version, and stack trace) may be collected through Google Play Console services to help us debug and resolve errors.

We Do NOT Collect: Names, postal addresses, phone numbers, contacts, microphone audio, GPS location, photos, financial payment information, or unique biometric identifiers.

03

Device Permissions

The App requests only strictly necessary permissions to deliver gameplay:

  • VIBRATE / HAPTICS: Provides tactile feedback when making correct pass connections, discovering clues, or completing tactical puzzles.
  • INTERNET / NETWORK STATE: Used solely to check for database updates, verify store license status via Google Play Services, and download optional official player facepacks. The gameplay itself does not require continuous internet connectivity.
04

Third-Party Services & SDKs

The App utilizes reputable, industry-standard third-party frameworks:

  • Google Play Services: Used for secure application distribution, verified app bundle updates, and crash analytics provided by Google LLC. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Expo & React Native: Open-source framework utilized to execute UI components and native bridging safely. (Expo Privacy Policy)
05

Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy Compliance)

FootIQ is a family-friendly educational sports logic puzzle game suitable for football fans of all ages. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect, request, or store any personal data from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).
  • The App contains no behavioral targeting, no aggressive user profiling, and no chat or communication features that expose young audiences to unknown parties.
  • If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with any information, please contact us at muhammetatmaca79@gmail.com, and we will promptly ensure any relevant diagnostic data is purged.
06

Data Retention, Security & Deletion Rights

Because FootIQ maintains local-first storage, you have 100% control over your data:

  • Immediate Data Deletion: You can completely and irreversibly wipe all high scores, streaks, settings, and progress by going to your device's Settings > Apps > FootIQ > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Data Security: We implement rigorous security best practices. Local databases are sandbox-isolated by the Android operating system, preventing unauthorized third-party apps from accessing your FootIQ game records.
07

Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Regulations)

Under European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users possess the right to:

  • Request verification on whether any personal data is held.
  • Request deletion of any diagnostic traces held by developer consoles.
  • Opt-out of diagnostic sharing through device-level privacy settings.
