Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how QR Base ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses your information in connection with your use of our mobile application.

QR Base is designed to be a fast, secure, and privacy-focused QR code scanner and generator. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information Collection

We prioritize your privacy. The app is designed to function with minimal data collection:

  • Camera Data: The app requires access to your device's camera to scan QR codes and barcodes. This data is processed in real-time on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Storage/Gallery: If you choose to scan an image from your gallery or save a generated QR code, we require permission to access your device storage.
  • No Personal Identification: We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or addresses.

2. Permissions

To provide its core functionality, QR Base requests the following permissions:

  • Camera: Used strictly for scanning codes.
  • Storage (Read/Write): Used to save generated QR codes or read QR codes from your gallery images.
  • Wi-Fi Connection: Used only if you scan a Wi-Fi QR code and choose to connect automatically.
  • Flashlight: Used to scan codes in low-light environments.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. The services used include:

  • Google Play Services: For core app functionality.
  • AdMob (if applicable): If the app contains ads, AdMob may collect data to serve relevant advertisements.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information (if any), thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

We do not sell this data. Learn how Google uses data: How Google uses information from partner sites or apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Personalised Ads & Consent (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your explicit consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), an IAB TCF v2 compliant consent form, before personal data is processed for advertising. If you do not consent, only non-personalised (limited) ads may be shown.

  • You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from the privacy/consent options inside the app.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • You can also turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings.

Your Rights
