Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information BeatDrop handles, why, and the choices you have.

Effective: 25 November 2026App: BeatDrop (com.virelon.beatdrop)

The short version

  • No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No camera and no microphone. The app does not record your voice.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The app shows ads. Our advertising partner, Google AdMob, may collect device data to show and measure ads.

Who we are

BeatDrop is developed and published by Virelonsoft ("we", "us"). Virelonsoft is responsible for this app. You can reach us at muhammetatmaca79@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, BeatDrop saves a small amount of data only on your device. It is not sent to us.

DataWhyWhere
Number of challenges you completedShown on the home screenYour device only

Deleting the app or clearing its storage removes this data.

Advertising

BeatDrop is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed or tapped, and diagnostic data

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time where the app offers this option.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

Permissions

  • Internet access: used to load ads.
  • Vibration: used for the optional beat vibration. You can turn it off on the home screen.

BeatDrop does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

Sharing

When you tap Challenge a friend, Android's share menu opens with a short message. You choose the app and the recipient; we do not see or store what you share.

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising described above, we do not share data with third parties.

Children

BeatDrop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, KVKK in Türkiye or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, most requests concern data held by Google for advertising; you can manage it through the controls above and Google's tools. For any question, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Security and retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete the app or its data. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app or on its store listing.

Reklamlar

BeatDrop ücretsizdir ve Google LLC'nin bir hizmeti olan Google AdMob reklamlarıyla desteklenir. AdMob, reklamları göstermek, sınırlamak, ölçmek ve dolandırıcılığı önlemek için şunları toplayıp işleyebilir:

  • Cihazının reklam kimliği (Android Reklam Kimliği)
  • Model, işletim sistemi, dil ve uygulama sürümü gibi cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri
  • IP adresi ve bundan çıkarılan yaklaşık konum
  • Görüntülenen veya dokunulan reklamlar gibi reklam etkileşimleri ve teşhis verileri

Google bu verileri kendi politikalarına göre işler: Google Gizlilik Politikası ve Google'ın, hizmetlerini kullanan uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullandığı.

  • Reklam kimliğini sıfırlayabilir veya silebilir ya da kişiselleştirilmiş reklamları kapatabilirsin: Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Reklamlar (yol cihaza göre değişebilir).
  • Kapatsan da reklam görmeye devam edersin, ancak reklamlar sana daha az uygun olur.
