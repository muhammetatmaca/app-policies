Privacy Policy

ClockAlarm

Developed by VirelonSoft • Effective Date: September 9, 2026

Summary: ClockAlarm respects your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. All alarm schedules and preferences are stored locally on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the ClockAlarm mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"), created and operated by VirelonSoft ("we", "us", or "our"). This document outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Application.

2. Information Collection and Storage

The Application is designed with a privacy-first approach:

  • Local Storage: Your alarms, custom labels, trigger conditions, and settings are saved exclusively on your local device using secure local storage (AsyncStorage). This data is never sent to or stored on VirelonSoft servers.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal identification details (such as your name, phone number, or social media accounts) to use the Application.
  • No Advertising Tracking: We do not track you across third-party apps or websites for targeted advertising purposes.

3. Permissions and How We Use Them

To function effectively as a smart, reliable alarm clock, the Application requests specific Android permissions. Each permission is strictly used for its intended feature:

SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM

Required to trigger alarms and notifications at the exact minute and second you scheduled, even when your device is in power-saving (Doze) mode.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Allows the Application to alert you with high-priority alarm notifications, sound warnings, and reminder banners when an alarm triggers.

RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Ensures that your active alarms and scheduled reminders are automatically restored and rescheduled after your phone restarts or reboots.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK

Enables uninterrupted alarm ringtone playback when your screen is locked or the Application is running in the background until dismissed or snoozed.

MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS

Allows the Application to configure audio output (such as routing to the main loudspeaker and playing over silent/do-not-disturb mode if configured) so that you never miss an alarm.

RECORD_AUDIO (Optional)

Used solely if audio recording or voice recognition alarm dismissal features are utilized. Audio is processed in real time on your device and is never recorded, saved, or transmitted to any external server.

4. Third-Party Services and APIs

To provide smart conditional alarm triggers (such as weather conditions, traffic estimates, crypto prices, and flight statuses), the Application may make anonymous requests to public APIs. These queries do not contain any personally identifiable information (PII) or device identifiers.

5. Data Security

Because all sensitive configuration and alarm information remains on your physical device, your data is protected by your device's built-in operating system security, sandbox model, and encryption.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can take necessary actions.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates are effective immediately upon posting the revised Privacy Policy on this page.
