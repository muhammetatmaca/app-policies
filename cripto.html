FULL PRIVACY POLICY

01. Scope & Purpose

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of VirelonSoft regarding the collection, storage, and handling of information when you use the ColdPass mobile application on iOS and Android operating systems.

02. Non-Custodial Nature & Cryptographic Keys

ColdPass does not generate, import, or store private keys, mnemonics, or recovery seeds. The application functions exclusively as an index and display pass for publicly verifiable blockchain wallet addresses. VirelonSoft has no technical access to any user assets.

03. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect: legal names, physical addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, location data (GPS), device identifiers (IDFA/AAID), or analytical tracking events.

04. On-Device Storage

User-provided public addresses and profile labels are stored locally in sandbox storage via React Native AsyncStorage. No remote server backup is established by VirelonSoft.

05. Blockchain & RPC Communications

To provide real-time token balances and gas prices in Gwei, ColdPass transmits read-only JSON-RPC requests directly to decentralized public blockchain endpoints. These requests do not pass through VirelonSoft servers.

06. Third-Party Libraries & SDKs

ColdPass does not embed third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics suites, or telemetry trackers. Open-source libraries are audited to ensure no unwanted telemetry is introduced.

07. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the absolute right to view, modify, and delete your data at any time. Because data is stored on-device, deleting the application permanently and irreversibly erases all local data.

08. Children's Privacy

ColdPass is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

09. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to revise this policy to reflect application enhancements. Updates are published directly on this official page with an updated effective date.
