🔒 100% Offline & Zero Personal Data Collection

ICD-10 Clinical Guide operates entirely offline on your device. Our application never collects, stores, transmits to external servers, or shares any personal identifiable information, clinical patient data, or device identifiers.

1. Overview and Purpose

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application ICD-10 Clinical Guide (package identifier: com.virelon.icd), developed and published by VirelonSoft ("we", "our", or "us").

We are firmly committed to user privacy and patient confidentiality. The application has been built from the ground up using a "Privacy-by-Design" architecture that requires zero personal data collection.

2. Data Collection and Processing

Regarding user and clinical data:

  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, identification numbers, or credentials.
  • Patient & Consultation Data: The application does not upload or process any patient health records, names, or clinical notes on external servers.
  • Device Identifiers: We do not collect IMEI, MAC addresses, Android Advertising IDs, or IP addresses.
  • Location Data: We do not request or collect precise or approximate geolocation data.

3. Local Device Storage

To provide a seamless workflow for physicians, the app stores minor configuration preferences strictly locally on your device (using Android SharedPreferences):

  • Your starred / favorite ICD-10 diagnosis codes for rapid lookup.
  • A boolean flag indicating onboarding completion.

This information never leaves your device. Clearing application data or uninstalling the app permanently purges all locally stored data.

4. Application Permissions

ICD-10 Clinical Guide requires no intrusive or sensitive system permissions:

  • ❌ Camera & Microphone: Not requested.
  • ❌ Contacts & Phone State: Not requested.
  • ❌ Geolocation / GPS: Not requested.
  • ❌ Storage / Media Access: Not requested.
  • ❌ Network / Internet: The entire WHO ICD-10 medical database is packaged locally; the app is fully functional with no internet connectivity.

Our application contains NO advertising SDKs (such as AdMob), NO user tracking frameworks, and NO third-party data analytics services. We do not sell, rent, or trade user information with data brokers or marketing platforms.
